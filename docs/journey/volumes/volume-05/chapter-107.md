## Chapter 107 — Harvest-first: the PM converges blind 4-of-5, six calculators, and the pr-review-toolkit lands mid-session (2026-06-14)

Chapter 106 closed at *decided-and-dispatched, not-yet-measured*: the bug PRs cooking, the PM running blind for the next five calculators. This chapter is the measurement returning — the convergence test resolving for the fourth iteration running, the operator's scope-and-sequence decision, and an unplanned arrival mid-session (a third-party PR-review plugin) that reshaped how the harvest got reviewed.

**The PM's blind five came back — and four of five matched the parked list.** The PM had been fired *blind* (parked candidates withheld, per the standing protocol traced back to Chapter 62). It returned a ranked set framed as *"a **consolidation-and-stress round** ... the highest-traffic calculator.net calculators that **recombine proven primitives at slightly higher complexity** — turning each primitive from 'demonstrated once' into 'demonstrated across a cluster'"* — and deliberately re-exercising the two open backend theories (#109/#110) and the FE stat-grid divergence (#143). Its five: **Loan (4)**, **Compound Interest (4)**, **Standard Deviation (3)**, **Right Triangle (3)**, **Date (3)**. The orchestrator laid the comparison out as a table:

| Parked candidate | PM blind pick | Match? |

| Compound Interest | Compound Interest (4) | ✅ |
| Right Triangle | Right Triangle (3) | ✅ |
| Std Dev | Standard Deviation (3) | ✅ |

*"Four of five converged independently — good signal the parked list was sound."* This is the fourth consecutive iteration where the blind PM re-derived most of an independently-parked shortlist (Chapter 62's BMI, Chapter 68's four-of-five for iter-0004, now this) — the convergence thesis accumulating its strongest run of evidence.

**The one divergence was a reasoned generalization, not a miss.** The PM chose **Loan** over the parked **Mortgage**, arguing Loan is *"the canonical generalization of Amortization (already built) — adds amortized/deferred/bond payment **modes** ... Unlocks the entire loan cluster (Auto Loan, Personal Loan, Student Loan, Business Loan, Mortgage) — proving Loan well makes that cluster near-mechanical."* It framed Mortgage (complexity 5) as the *"higher-risk stress build to do **after** Loan proves the cluster"* — stacking charts + schedule + extra-payments all at once. The PM didn't drop Mortgage; it re-sequenced it behind a primitive that subsumes it.

**The operator's decision: six calculators, harvest-first.** Rather than pick between Loan and Mortgage, the operator took both — and added the discipline that the bug fixes land *before* any build. The orchestrator recorded it: *"Locked in: **6 calculators** (Loan + Mortgage + Compound Interest + Standard Deviation + Right Triangle + Date), **harvest-first**. That means we land the open fixes before any calculator build, so the new calcs inherit clean machinery and this iteration actually *tests* the harvest."* The harvest-first ordering is itself a test the PM had flagged as a *lever*: Loan and Compound Interest take term-in-years/count inputs that re-trigger #109's integer coercion, and the math calcs re-trigger #110's non-numeric→0 — so merging the bug fixes first turns the new iteration into a real test of whether the harvest *holds* rather than a re-observation of the bugs. Similarly, harvesting #143 (the shared stat-grid) first means Standard Deviation and Compound Interest's new stat-grid pages inherit one component rather than widening the six-way divergence.

**The harvest board took shape.** The orchestrator named the gating discipline explicitly — *"everything below must go green and merge before the calculator fan-out opens"* — and tracked four in-flight harvest items: #111 SimpleCov (PR #172), #109/#110 numeric coercion (backend agent building), #143 shared stat-grid (frontend agent building), and the historian's triage/kickoff journal. It also re-named the deferred durable half: *"**I codify the #109/#110 convention into `.claude/agents/backend.md`** — the durable half the build agents can't write — so the next regen doesn't erase it."* The build fan-out (12 issues — backend + frontend for each of the 6, sequenced Loan → Mortgage) was explicitly held until the harvest closes and `iteration-NNNN` is pinned at the post-harvest SHA.

**The unplanned arrival: the pr-review-toolkit plugin.** Mid-harvest, the operator ran `/reload-plugins` and asked: *"Do you have visibility into the pr-review-toolkit?"* After the reload it was fully visible — a plugin contributing **one skill + six specialized review agents**: `review-pr` (the orchestrating skill), and the agents `code-reviewer`, `code-simplifier`, `comment-analyzer`, `pr-test-analyzer`, `silent-failure-hunter`, and `type-design-analyzer`. The orchestrator placed it precisely against the existing machinery: *"it's a *correctness/design code-review* layer, distinct from our `quality-assurance-agent`, which is a *pre-merge gate* (CI + visual/screenshot + DB-persistence). They're complementary — QA proves it's green and on-brand; this toolkit critiques the code itself."*

**It flagged a contract nuance up front.** Before using the toolkit, the orchestrator named exactly how it sits relative to the experiment's agent-first contract: *"these are **generic** review agents — they read CLAUDE.md but aren't part of our agent-first contract (no worktree discipline, not pinned to an iteration). So I'd treat them as advisory review tooling, not a contract gate, unless you want to formally adopt them (that'd be a harvest decision)."* This is the same defined-vs-adopted distinction that ran through Chapters 102–104 (the QA + github agents): a capability can be present without being part of the contract until a deliberate harvest adopts it. The operator chose to use the toolkit live — *"Yep fire all 3"* — pointing `silent-failure-hunter` and `pr-test-analyzer` at #174 and `code-reviewer` at #143's PR (#176) once it landed. The next two chapters record what those reviews caught.

**Agent/doc-driven? ✅ (with a new tool entering advisory, not contract, status).** The new-build set came through the PM run blind, preserving the convergence measurement; the operator's six-calc/harvest-first decision is pure sequencing. The pr-review-toolkit was adopted *as advisory tooling* with the contract distinction named clearly — it is not (yet) part of the agent-first gate, and its eventual integration was queued as a harvest decision (the issue filed in Chapter 109). The harvest-first ordering is notable as deliberate experimental design: it makes the upcoming iteration a *test* of whether the harvested fixes survive, not a re-run of the bugs.
