## Chapter 117 — Iteration-0007 reframed as a process stress test: the locked 10, and one deliberate capability nudge (2026-06-15)

This chapter records the design of the next iteration — and a correction the operator made to the orchestrator's framing that changed *what iteration-0007 is for*. It is the next-iteration setup half of the close-out: the locked 10-calculator set, the deliberate decision to keep one new-primitive probe, and the persistence of all of it to operator memory as the next session's starting point.

**The orchestrator's first framing — and the operator's sharper one.** Coming out of the capability reflection (Chapter 116), the orchestrator described iteration-0007 (10 calculators) as a *consistency* round: *"it holds capability constant and probes the two open questions — *did the harvest take* and *is 'acceptable' reproducible*."* The operator confirmed the domain would stay fixed — *"Ha, I mean it'll still be calculators, but now it'll be 10. I'm testing the learnings we had on the proces and also consistency in acceptable results"* — and the orchestrator affirmed that holding the domain constant was good experimental hygiene (*"changing the domain *and* the scale at once would muddy both readings"*).

But then the operator reframed the *purpose* of the iteration in a way the orchestrator immediately recognized as sharper than its own:

> **"Wouldn't it then not be a good idea to say that the next iteration is *only* upping the count to 10? The variability changes from more results to more process stress test. We're just testing something new. This past time there was nothing wrong with the result that we didn't fix, and otherwise it was process. So now, stress test that"**

The orchestrator accepted the correction explicitly — *"You're right, and it's a sharper framing than mine — let me correct course."* — and restated the thesis it had missed: iteration-0006's evidence is that *"**results were the non-issue**: nothing shipped wrong that we didn't catch and fix, and the operator review came back clean. *Everything* that strained was process — coordination, shared state, orchestration at fan-out. So iteration-0007 isn't really 'more results to check for consistency'; the variable genuinely under test is **process robustness at higher load** — ~20 build PRs, 20+ gates, the merge/rebase tail, the registry/fixture coordination — now that the harvest supposedly hardened all of it. Result-consistency rides along as a passive confirmation, but **the stress test is the point.** We're testing something new (the machinery at scale), not re-proving the output."*

**Why this reframe is the load-bearing move.** It turns iteration-0007 from a results round into a **regression test of the iteration-0006 harvest itself**. The conventions that round encoded — INVENTORY-backfill-at-Open, the derived calculator count, fixture-with-page atomicity, the registry cross-seam prohibition, `::Date`/`::Time` qualification, boundary-safe date tests — are now *in the pinned agent set*. The orchestrator had already named the test cleanly earlier in the exchange: *"10 parallel builds will either hit the same coordination walls (the harvest didn't take) or glide past them (it did),"* and the single most valuable preparation is to *"write the predictions into the iteration log up front … Then a recurrence is a clean *falsification*, not a vague 'huh, that again.'"* It also proposed two consistency numbers to track — **first-pass gate rate** (PRs that pass QA without rework) and **notes/no-notes per calculator** — so the passive results-consistency reading is legible too.

**The PM ran blind and returned the 10.** Per the experiment's A/B discipline, the orchestrator dispatched the PM *blind* (not shown the reflection), framed for the stress test — *"favoring proven-primitive recombinations so results stay controlled and process/scale is the isolated variable; it'll flag any non-recombinations for you to swap out."* The PM came back with a ranked set explicitly tuned to **hold results constant**: **9 pure recombinations** of the 14-calculator proven primitive set — Auto Loan, Sales Tax, Pythagorean Theorem, Discount, VAT, Personal Loan, Average Return, ROI, House Affordability — *"every one maps onto a primitive already shipped and operator-approved across iterations 0003–0006. No new chart type, no new input shape, no new math pattern."* It even built in a deliberate twin-probe: VAT is *"intentionally a structural near-twin"* of Sales Tax *"to make the fan-out itself the variable"* — two structurally-identical calcs to test whether the agents converge them consistently.

The tenth, **Income Tax**, the PM flagged as the odd one out: *"#10 Income Tax introduces ONE new primitive: piecewise/bracket math (tax brackets are a step-function lookup the catalog hasn't built yet)."* Its recommendation was to swap it for a clean drop-in — its default being **Boat Loan** (*"keeps a 4th loan-family member to really exercise the just-harvested loan primitive under fan-out"*) or **Margin** — so the stress test stays uncontaminated by a capability variable. The PM also gave a shallow dependency graph: build **Auto Loan first** to re-establish the loan-family primitive at the pinned agents, after which Personal Loan, House Affordability (the Mortgage core *"run 'backwards'"* — DTI → max payment → max loan), and any swapped-in loan skin fan out trivially; everything else is independent parallel work — *"ideal load for a ~20-PR scale test."*

**The one deliberate nudge — the operator keeps Income Tax.** The orchestrator surfaced the PM's swap recommendation as a clean call. The operator chose **against** it:

> **"Locked: Income Tax stays"** — recorded by the orchestrator as *"a deliberate single capability nudge (bracket math) riding inside the process stress test. So the iteration-0007 set is **9 pure recombinations + Income Tax as the one conscious new-primitive probe**."*

This is a notable operator decision for the log: rather than keeping the stress test perfectly clean (the PM's hygienic recommendation), the operator chose to ride **one conscious capability probe** inside the scale test — bracket/piecewise math, the first genuinely-new primitive the catalog hasn't built. It directly answers the orchestrator's Chapter 116 suggestion to *"aim the next round somewhere less forgiving"* — not by leaving calculators, but by smuggling a single new primitive into an otherwise-controlled process round. The trade-off is exactly as the PM warned: a "miss" on Income Tax will be ambiguous (process gap vs. new ground), but the operator chose that ambiguity deliberately, as one probe among ten controlled builds.

**Persistence to memory as the next-session starting point.** Per the operator's instruction — *"swap it into your memory in replacement of the previous 6, mark that we'll start there next time"* — the orchestrator retired the stale parked-calculators note and wrote a new memory file, `iteration-0007-plan.md`, holding the locked 10 (9 recombinations + Income Tax as the deliberate nudge), the **process-stress-test framing** (2× fan-out, ~20 build PRs), the guidance to **write predictions into the log up front** (so a recurrence of 0006's coordination issues is a clean falsification that the harvest didn't take), the **consistency metrics** (first-pass gate rate + notes/no-notes), and a flag that it is the next session's starting point. The memory index was updated to point at the new plan and clear the now-superseded "start here" from the stale triage note.

The experiment that opens next session is not "more calculators" — it is, by the operator's own correction, *"stress test that."*
